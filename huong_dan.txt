HUONG DAN CHAY VTON PIPELINE TREN RTX 5070 (NET) - BAN CAP NHAT
==================================================================
(thay the ban HUONG_DAN_NET_5070.txt cu - da doi nhieu so voi ban cu)

NHUNG GI DA THAY DOI SO VOI BAN CU, CAN BIET TRUOC:
------------------------------------------------------
- KHONG CAN calibrate tay / --mannequin_id nua. generate.py gio TU DONG
  detect pose (vai/hong/goi/dau/chan) ngay tren anh mannequin_bare, dung
  chung 1 co che voi luc chuan bi dataset. --mannequin_id chi con la
  phuong an du phong (chi ho tro top/bottom, khong ho tro hat/shoes/dress).
- THEM 3 LOAI ITEM MOI: hat (mu), shoes (giay), dress (vay lien). Tong
  cong gio la 5 loai: hat, top, bottom, shoes, dress.
- CO CHE MAC NHIEU MON CUNG LUC: generate.py gio nhan --hat/--top/--bottom
  /--shoes/--dress (chon bao nhieu tuy y, tru dress khong duoc di cung
  top/bottom) va tu dong mac tuan tu tat ca trong 1 lan goi.
- requirements.txt DA CO SAN diffusers/transformers/accelerate/peft - KHONG
  CAN pip install rieng nhu ban cu nua (do thua, khong hai nhung khong can).
- --no-gradient_checkpointing gio MOI THUC SU HOAT DONG (ban cu bi bug
  argparse, flag nay truoc day khong co tac dung du co ghi trong lenh).
- prepare_dataset.py gio TU TAI 1 model pose (~30MB tu Google) o lan chay
  dau tien - CAN INTERNET luc do, chi tai 1 lan roi cache lai.


0. TRUOC KHI ROI NHA (tren may co code da sua)
-----------------------------------------------
cd vton_pipeline
git add .
git commit -m "vton pipeline v2 - 5 item categories, auto pose-detect"
git push origin main

Luu y: raw_data/, data/, lora_output*/ khong nam trong git (.gitignore loai ra).
Mang rieng qua USB/Google Drive.


1. CLONE REPO VE MAY NET
-------------------------
git clone https://github.com/<user>/<repo>.git
cd <repo>


2. TAO VENV
-----------
python -m venv .venv
.venv\Scripts\activate


3. CAI PYTORCH DUNG BAN CHO RTX 5070 (Blackwell, sm_120, can torch >= 2.7)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
pip install torch torchvision torchaudio --index-url https://download.pytorch.org/whl/cu128


4. CAI THU VIEN CON LAI (gio chi 1 dong duy nhat)
----------------------------------------------------
pip install -r requirements.txt


5. KIEM TRA GPU
----------------
python -c "import torch; print(torch.__version__, torch.cuda.is_available(), torch.cuda.get_device_name(0))"

Phai thay: version >= 2.7, True, ten card la RTX 5070.
Neu sai ten card / False -> dung lai kiem tra driver NVIDIA truoc khi lam tiep.


6. COPY RAW_DATA VAO DUNG VI TRI
----------------------------------
Copy toan bo raw_data (tu USB/Drive) vao:
<repo>\raw_data\sample_0001\...
<repo>\raw_data\sample_0002\...
...

Moi sample co the co BAT KY to hop nao trong 5 loai:
  garment_hat.*, garment_top.*, garment_bottom.*, garment_shoes.*, garment_dress.*
(khong bat buoc du ca 5 - 1 sample chi co garment_hat la van hop le)
LUU Y: garment_dress KHONG duoc di kem garment_top/garment_bottom trong
cung 1 sample (se bi tu dong skip, xem ly do trong log [skip]).

Kiem tra: dir raw_data  -> phai thay liet ke sample_0001, sample_0002, ...


7. CHUAN BI DATASET (FULL 512PX)
-----------------------------------
python phase2_diffusion/prepare_dataset.py --raw_dir raw_data --out_dir data/train --image_size 512

Lan dau chay se in dong "[pose_detect] Downloading pose model..." - can
internet, doi vai giay. Doc ky cac dong [skip] (neu co) de biet sample nao
bi loai va vi sao truoc khi train.


8. TRAIN FULL CONFIG (TAN DUNG 12GB VRAM CUA 5070)
--------------------------------------------------------------
accelerate launch phase2_diffusion/train_lora.py `
  --data_dir data/train --output_dir lora_output_final `
  --resolution 512 --train_batch_size 6 --gradient_accumulation_steps 1 `
  --no-gradient_checkpointing --lora_rank 32 --num_train_epochs 40 `
  --learning_rate 1e-4 --save_every 5

Neu OOM (bao "CUDA out of memory"): giam dan theo thu tu nay
  1) --train_batch_size 4
  2) bo --no-gradient_checkpointing (de mac dinh bat lai)
  3) --lora_rank 16


9. GENERATE THU - MAC NHIEU MON CUNG LUC (CACH MOI)
--------------------------------------------------------
python phase2_diffusion/generate.py `
  --lora_path lora_output_final/final `
  --mannequin_bare raw_data/sample_0001/mannequin_bare.jpg `
  --top raw_data/sample_0001/garment_top.png `
  --bottom raw_data/sample_0001/garment_bottom.png `
  --sleeve_length auto `
  --n_variants 3 --out_dir results/

(KHONG can --mannequin_id nua - tu dong detect. Neu muon test rieng 1 mon,
chi dien dung 1 trong --hat/--top/--bottom/--shoes/--dress la du.)

Vi du mac du hat+top+bottom+shoes trong 1 lan:
python phase2_diffusion/generate.py `
  --lora_path lora_output_final/final `
  --mannequin_bare raw_data/sample_0001/mannequin_bare.jpg `
  --hat item/cap.jpg --top item/shirt.jpg --bottom item/jeans.jpg --shoes item/sneaker.jpg `
  --n_variants 3 --out_dir results_full/


10. SAO CHEP KET QUA RA NGOAI NGAY - QUAN TRONG NHAT
------------------------------------------------------
Copy toan bo lora_output_final\ va results\ ra Google Drive/USB
TRUOC KHI ROI MAY. May net thuong xoa du lieu giua cac phien thue.
